# Privacy

The EcoLogits extension keeps your data in your browser: it stores no message content, only metadata and impacts, sends nothing anywhere and has no telemetry.

Read the full privacy policy at <https://adr1enr1chard.github.io/ecologits-extension/privacy/> ([en français](https://adr1enr1chard.github.io/ecologits-extension/fr/privacy/)).
