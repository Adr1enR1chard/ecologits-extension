Download and install

The extension is not on the browser stores yet. Download the zip for your browser from the latest release, then load it by hand.

Chromium · v{{version}}
  1. Unzip the downloaded file.
  2. Open chrome://extensions.
  3. Turn on Developer mode, top right.
  4. Click Load unpacked and pick the unzipped folder.

For Chromium-based browsers: tested on Chrome and Edge, and should work in Brave, Opera, Vivaldi… Their extensions page also opens from chrome://extensions.

Firefox · v{{version}}
  1. Open about:debugging.
  2. Click This Firefox.
  3. Click Load Temporary Add-on… and pick the downloaded zip.

Temporary on Firefox: the add-on is removed when Firefox restarts, and must be loaded again.

Features

  • A badge under each reply

    Its estimated energy, GHG emissions and water, with the min–max range on hover. It can be turned off in the settings.

  • A popup with your totals

    Today, 7 days, 30 days and all time, by provider and model, with everyday equivalents from ADEME Impact CO2, abiotic resources and primary energy, split into usage and embodied impacts.

  • Flags on uncertain estimates

    A lower bound, a stopped reply or an approximated model: the figures never read as more exact than they are.

  • English and French

    The extension follows your browser's language.

Flags on uncertain estimates

When an estimate is less certain, a symbol next to the badge says why. Hover it in the extension for the explanation.

  • 2.9 Wh·1.22 gCO₂eq·5.22 mL ≥

    Lower-bound estimate

    The model reasoned without showing all of its reasoning: hidden reasoning tokens are not counted, so the actual impact is higher.

  • 0.61 Wh·0.256 gCO₂eq·1.1 mL …

    Stopped reply

    The reply was stopped before its end: only the part generated until then is counted.

  • 8.9 Wh·3.74 gCO₂eq·16 mL ≈

    Approximated model

    The model used by the web app is not exposed or unknown to EcoLogits: the impact is computed with a comparable model, or as a range between two models.

How it works

  1. Read the reply

    While the reply streams in, the extension reads a copy of it, leaving the page untouched.

  2. Count the tokens

    It counts the tokens the model generated, and notes which model it was.

  3. Compute the impacts

    EcoLogits turns the model and the token count into energy, GHG emissions, water and more, with a min–max range.

Privacy

Your data stays in your browser. The extension stores no message content, only metadata and impacts, sends nothing anywhere and has no telemetry. You can export or delete everything from its settings.
