Privacy policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

This policy covers the EcoLogits browser extension and this website. In short: your data stays in your browser, no message content is stored, nothing is sent anywhere, and there is no telemetry.

What the extension reads

On ChatGPT (chatgpt.com), Claude (claude.ai) and Gemini (gemini.google.com), the extension reads a copy of each reply as it streams in, and on some providers of the request that asked for it, to know which model answered and how many tokens it generated. The page itself is left untouched.

The text of the reply, and its reasoning when the provider shows it, is only used to count tokens, inside your browser; the request is only used to find the model. Both are then discarded: they are never stored and never leave your browser.

What the extension stores

Everything is kept in the browser's extension storage, on your device only:

  • For each of your last 500 replies: the provider, the model shown by the web app and the model used for the estimate, the reply's identifier on the provider's site, the time, the number of output tokens, the generation time, the estimated impacts, and the flags and warnings of the estimate.
  • Daily totals of the impacts and of the number of replies, per provider and model.
  • For each provider, when the last reply was captured, and when the last capture failed, with the technical error.
  • Your settings, such as whether the badge is shown.

It never stores the content of your messages: neither your prompts nor the replies.

What the extension sends

Nothing. The extension has no server and no account, makes no network request of its own, and collects no analytics or telemetry. The impacts are computed inside your browser with the EcoLogits library, which ships with the extension.

Export and delete your data

From the extension's settings, you can:

  • export the daily totals, or your last 500 replies, as CSV;
  • export everything as JSON;
  • delete all data, at once.

The exported files are saved on your device and go nowhere else. Removing the extension also deletes all of its data.

Permissions

The extension asks for the storage permission, to keep the data above, and runs only on the providers' sites listed above.

This website

This site uses no analytics and no cookies, and loads nothing from third parties: no fonts, no scripts, no trackers. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, and the downloads are served by GitHub Releases. GitHub may log technical data about the requests, such as your IP address, as described in the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Changes and contact

Changes to this policy are published on this page, and its history is kept in the source repository. For any question, open an issue.
